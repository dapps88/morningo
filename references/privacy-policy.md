**The Meta Pixel**

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a piece of code provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. When you accept cookies, the Meta Pixel loads and may set cookies in your browser. This allows Meta to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to help us reach people with similar interests through Facebook and Instagram.

Meta may use data collected through the Pixel in accordance with their own Data Policy, available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

To manage how Meta uses your data for advertising, visit your Meta Ad Preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

We do not use the Meta Pixel to collect data from anyone we know to be under the age of 13.

**Cookies**

Cookies are small text files saved to your browser by websites you visit.

This site uses two types:

Strictly necessary cookies — required for the site to function. Set regardless of your preference. No personal data is collected through these.

Analytics and advertising cookies — set by GA4 and the Meta Pixel. Only loaded if you accept cookies when prompted. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or adjusting your browser settings.
