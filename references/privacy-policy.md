## PAGE COPY

**Privacy Policy**

Last updated: 4 October 2026

---

**Who we are**

This site is operated by Daniel Appelbee, based in the United Kingdom.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at wearemorningo@gmail.com.

---

**What we collect and why**

When you register your interest via this site, we collect your email address. We use it for one purpose only: to let you know if and when Morningo launches. We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or any other promotional content, and we will not build an ongoing mailing list from it. If you arrived from one of our adverts, we also save which advert and campaign brought you here, along with your email address, so we can see which adverts are working.

If you use the "Have a question?" box, we save the question you type when you press Send message, even if you do not add an email address. If you do add your email address, we use it only to reply to you. Please do not include health details or other sensitive personal information in your question.

Our host, Netlify, automatically records technical details such as your IP address and browser type when you visit the site or send a form.

When you visit this site, we may also collect usage data and advertising measurement data through cookies, if you have given your consent. See the Cookies section below for details.

---

**Legal basis for processing**

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the registration form.

We process cookie and tracking data on the basis of your consent, given when you accept cookies via the banner on this site.

We process questions, the advert information saved with them, and technical details on the basis of our legitimate interests in answering questions, understanding which adverts work, and keeping the site secure.

You can withdraw consent at any time. See Your Rights below.

---

**The Meta Pixel**

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a piece of code provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. When you accept cookies, the Meta Pixel loads and may set cookies in your browser. This allows Meta to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to help us reach people with similar interests through Facebook and Instagram.

Meta may use data collected through the Pixel in accordance with their own Data Policy, available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

To manage how Meta uses your data for advertising, visit your Meta Ad Preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

We do not use the Meta Pixel to collect data from anyone we know to be under the age of 13.

---

**Google Analytics**

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google LLC. When you accept cookies, GA4 may set cookies in your browser to help us understand how visitors find and use this site. This data is pseudonymised and used in aggregate: it is linked to a random identifier stored in a cookie, not to your name or email address, and we do not use it to identify you.

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

---

**Who we share your data with**

We do not sell your data. We do not share your email address with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

The following services process data on our behalf as data processors:

- Netlify: hosts this site and receives form submissions (netlify.com/privacy)
- Google: analytics via GA4 (policies.google.com/privacy)
- Meta: advertising measurement via Meta Pixel (facebook.com/privacy/policy)

Each operates under their own privacy policy and is contractually required to handle your data appropriately.

---

**International data transfers**

Some of our data processors are based outside the UK. Where this is the case, we ensure transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, including the UK-US Data Bridge where applicable.

---

**Cookies**

Cookies are small text files saved to your browser by websites you visit.

This site uses two types:

Strictly necessary cookies: one cookie that remembers your cookie choice. It is set whichever button you press and lasts 12 months. No personal data is collected through it.

Analytics and advertising cookies: set by GA4 and the Meta Pixel. Only loaded if you accept cookies when prompted. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page. If you decline after accepting, we remove the analytics and advertising cookies we have set.

| Cookie | Set by | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `morningo-consent` | This site | Remembers your cookie choice | 12 months |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells visitors apart so we can count visits | Up to 2 years (Chrome limits it to about 13 months) |
| `_ga_33SVXEVNJP` | Google Analytics | Remembers the current visit | Up to 2 years (Chrome limits it to about 13 months) |

(On the page each cookie is shown as its own stacked block so it reads on a phone. Add `_fbp` and `_fbc`, Meta, 3 months, when the Meta Pixel is switched on.)

---

**How long we keep your data**

We will keep your email address for up to 24 months from the date you register, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever is sooner.

We keep questions, and the advert information saved with them, for the same period.

To stop receiving communications from us, or to ask us to delete your data, contact us at wearemorningo@gmail.com.

Cookie data held by Google and Meta is subject to their own retention policies.

---

**Your rights**

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Ask us to correct inaccurate data
- Ask us to delete your data
- Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting any processing carried out before withdrawal
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, email us at wearemorningo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

---

**Changes to this policy**

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
