# Redesign v3 (local variant)

Started 2026-11-06 on the `redesign` branch. `concepts/product-wireframe-v3.html` is a copy of the live product page (`product-wireframe-v2.html`, which is untouched) with the changes the owner worked out on a design canvas. Nothing here is live: `_redirects` still sends `/home` to v2, v3 is marked `noindex`, and the branch has not been pushed.

View it: `python3 scripts/dev-server.py 8744 concepts`, then http://localhost:8744/product-wireframe-v3.html. The live page is the same address ending `-v2.html`, so the two can be compared side by side.

## What changed against the live page

- **Nav:** soft warm glow behind the logo (stops at 50% strength), faint white key line along the bottom, basket on the far right, "Question" as plain underlined text beside it, basket and Question in the logo cream `#f9f8c1`.
- **Hero copy:** title "Morning energy drink"; flavour line directly under it; new intro; "The core ingredients" sub-head gone; four bullets with a bold lead ("Support concentration", "Reduce tiredness", "Stay relaxed and switched on", "Keep mental fatigue at bay").
- **Hero body text:** 16px on 26px lines in near-black `#17150F` (after the Feel page), in the hero and the product section. Accordion titles, sub-headings and panel text match.
- **Hero buttons and pills:** the "Early access offer" pill and the grey "Limited first release" box are gone. The button says "Buy now" in `#581E85`. Two soft capsules under it: "30 day money back guarantee" and "Free shipping".

Modelled on the strip on mengotomars.com. The page column closes before it and reopens after (the same pattern as the nav and footer), and a small script copies the group until the track is wider than the screen. Regular weight, 1px letter spacing, 24px lines so two-line items are not cramped, 48px between items.

Links in plum and the plum comparison card's white text are unchanged. Small grey captions (the italic labels on the ingredient cards, footnotes, sticky caption) and the nutrition modal are unchanged. Line spacing outside the hero and product section is still the old 1.45.
- **Questions section:** the first accordion, "It's not on sale yet. What am I signing up for?", is removed (owner, 2026-11-06). The other three stay, all closed.
- **Gallery:** the progress bar on the main image is gone (the thumbnails still show the active picture). Six new pictures (the owner's numbered 1 to 6, 2000x1500 WebP) replace the live page's five plus video, in the order from the owner's design frames Hero1 to Hero6: 1 box with four benefits, 2 the two reviews (Amber and Loic), 3 "Could replace your morning coffee...", 4 "Mix with water...", 5 "Great for days when you need your head down", 6 "Won't help you sleep...". Files `gallery-v3-1` to `gallery-v3-6`, resized to 1600x1200 and re-encoded at quality 82 with the same settings as `scripts/optimize-images.sh` (landscape, 4:3). ## "One moment…" on Buy now (was "Checking stock"), and the pages after the waitlist

- **Buy now, pressed (owner, 2026-11-06):** all three Buy now buttons (hero, product section, sticky bar) turn into a small spinner and "One moment…" for 1.6 seconds, then move on to the waitlist page. The button keeps its size and purple (not the dimmed look other disabled buttons get), ignores a second press, tells screen readers "One moment…", and is put right again if the visitor comes back with the Back button. The text (`LOADING_LABEL`) and the pause (`WAIT_MS`) are two constants in the product page's script. **Wording change (owner, 2026-11-06):** it first said "Checking stock…"; the owner chose the neutral "One moment…" because a stock check that always ends the same way implies a lookup that does not happen (UK availability and unfair-practice rules, see the go-live notes). Options considered and not used: "Taking you to the waitlist…" (most honest), "Opening your place…". Avoid wording that implies a real lookup or transaction: "Checking availability…", "Looking for stock…", "Finding your sachets…", "Reserving…", "Processing…". The pause could be cut to about 0.6 seconds (enough for the click to be counted) if the owner wants it. The click is still counted by analytics.js.
- **The journey, in order:** `product-wireframe-v3.html` (Buy now) → `email-capture-v3.html` (Join waitlist) → `about-you-v3.html` (the two-question survey; Done or Skip) → `thank-you-v3.html` (copy a link to share). Each logo and back link inside the redesign leads back to the redesigned product page.
- **Survey and share pages (`about-you-v3.html`, `thank-you-v3.html`):** copies of the live pages (untouched) with the dark glow nav and the primary button in the same purple as Buy now (Done on the survey, Copy link on the share page). Everything else is unchanged: questions, the gold "You're on the list" pill, saving to the same Netlify forms, tracking, and the plum footer. The survey's selected answer cards still use the old plum ring (not asked to change). (The question dialog's "Send message" buttons were still plum here; changed on 2026-11-06, see "Question box and privacy page" below.)
- **Sticky Done on phones (owner, 2026-11-06):** on the survey page the answers fill more than one screen, so Done (and Skip under it) were out of view. On screens under 768px they now sit in a bar stuck to the bottom of the screen (hairline and soft shadow only while floating; in their normal place once the form's end is reached). Tested at 390x844 and 375x667: Done is fully in view on load, the last answer row is clear of the bar at the end, Done with nothing answered keeps the page, marks both groups and brings the first one into view clear of the nav and the bar, and answering then pressing Done still moves on. On desktop nothing is stuck.
- **Share page headline (owner, 2026-11-06):** "Thanks, share with friends" (was "You're in, share with friends"); the browser tab title is now "Morningo Thanks (redesign)". The line under it is unchanged.
- **Opening each page at the top (owner, 2026-11-06):** on the phone test link, a page opened from a longer page (waitlist from the product page, share page from the survey) landed at its bottom. On a normal browser, and on the local site, it does not: pressing Buy now at scroll 2,596px lands on the waitlist page at 0. The cause is the test link's wrapper, which keeps the surrounding page's scroll position when the next page opens, so a shorter page clamps to its bottom (reproduced in a stand-in wrapper: 256px of 256px). All four redesigned pages now carry a small script (before attribution.js) that, when a page is opened by navigating to it (not Back, not a reload), scrolls to the top and calls `scrollIntoView` so the wrapper's scroll goes up too; it stands down once the visitor has touched, scrolled or typed. In the stand-in wrapper the same test now lands at 0. Not seen on a real phone: if the link still lands low, tell the assistant.
- **The link people copy** on the share page is still the live site's address (`https://morningo.netlify.app/home`, tagged as a share), which is the OLD product page until the redesign replaces it. That is how the page was built on purpose; it is not changed here.
