What we collect and why

When you register your interest via this site, we collect your email address. We use it for one purpose only: to let you know if and when Morningo launches. We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or any other promotional content, and we will not build an ongoing mailing list from it. We collect nothing else.

When you visit this site, we may also collect anonymised usage data and advertising measurement data through cookies, if you have given your consent. See the Cookies section below for details.

Legal basis for processing

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the registration form.

We process cookie and tracking data on the basis of your consent, given when you accept cookies via the banner on this site.

You can withdraw consent at any time. See Your Rights below.

The Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a piece of code provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. When you accept cookies, the Meta Pixel loads and may set cookies in your browser. This allows Meta to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to help us reach people with similar interests through Facebook and Instagram.

Meta may use data collected through the Pixel in accordance with their own Data Policy, available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

To manage how Meta uses your data for advertising, visit your Meta Ad Preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

We do not use the Meta Pixel to collect data from anyone we know to be under the age of 13.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google LLC. When you accept cookies, GA4 may set cookies in your browser to help us understand how visitors find and use this site. This data is anonymised and aggregated. It does not identify you personally.

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data. We do not share your email address with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

The following services process data on our behalf as data processors:

  • Netlify — hosts this site and receives form submissions (netlify.com/privacy)
  • Google — analytics via GA4 (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Meta — advertising measurement via Meta Pixel (facebook.com/privacy/policy)

Each operates under their own privacy policy and is contractually required to handle your data appropriately.

International data transfers

Some of our data processors are based outside the UK. Where this is the case, we ensure transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, including the UK-US Data Bridge where applicable.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files saved to your browser by websites you visit.

This site uses two types:

Strictly necessary cookies — required for the site to function. Set regardless of your preference. No personal data is collected through these.

Analytics and advertising cookies — set by GA4 and the Meta Pixel. Only loaded if you accept cookies when prompted. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or adjusting your browser settings.

How long we keep your data

We will keep your email address for up to 24 months from the date you register, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever is sooner.

To stop receiving communications from us, or to ask us to delete your data, contact us at [email address].

Cookie data held by Google and Meta is subject to their own retention policies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting any processing carried out before withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [email address]. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy
